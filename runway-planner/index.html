How the numbers work. Starting cash is the 25 Oct 2026 bank balance, and the plan starts in November, so October's costs are treated as already paid. Revenue and funding are counted in full in the month they land, with no GST or tax adjustments. "Expected" multiplies each prospect by its likelihood.

Projections. New deals are spread evenly across each quarter. Recurring deals renew on each 12-month anniversary at the renewal rate. A pay rise counts only the increase over the current salary. Super is added at the rate set; payroll tax and workers' compensation aren't included. ARR means annual recurring revenue; ACV means annual contract value.

Sources. Costs, wages, balance and the Snow $200K come from Miks' notes. Funding notes come from Notion (Snow Foundation CRM record, 18 Aug Snow meeting, 19 Oct director catch-up). Supabase and Notion price notes are from public pricing guides. Check them before relying on them.
