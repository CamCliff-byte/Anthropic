# Working with Cam (Capyble)

Notes on how Cam likes to work, learnt from sessions. Update when Cam gives strong feedback or a session closes.

## Communication
- Australian English. Plain, simple language. Lead with the outcome.
- No compliments or agreement unless asked. Don't assert without evidence; flag inferences.
- Use Cam's own wording. Don't add sentences of your own to their copy; if you must, say so.
- When Cam deletes something from a source doc, it should not reappear in anything built from that doc.

## Proposals and design pages
- Prefers vertical A4 pages Cam can edit directly over 16:9 slide decks.
- On a Design canvas, every block should be freely draggable (absolutely positioned), not locked into flex or grid stacks. Cards keep their contents together.
- Print must be right: consistent margins, footer about 40px from the bottom edge, nothing running past the page edge. Measure the rendered page with the real fonts before saying it fits.
- Don't use empty "spacer" elements on a Design canvas: the editor gives them a size and pushes content off the page.
- Prefers real images over placeholders: stills pulled from the linked videos, front-on photos rather than odd angles, and Tash's illustrations to fill blank space.
- Cam edits the canvas live while Claude works. Re-read files right before publishing and rebuild on the latest version; never overwrite their edits.
- Unused pages go to an "Archive" canvas page rather than being deleted.
